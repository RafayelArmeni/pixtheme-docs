# Ready-made sections

Sections are pre-built blocks you can add to any Openhouse page. In the page editor, click **+ Add**, open **Sections**, and drag one onto the page. Then edit it like any other module.

| Section | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Hero with property search** | Large photo hero with a search bar for location, type and price |
| **Featured properties** | Property cards with photo, price, status badge and key facts |
| **Services** | Cards for buying, selling, renting or any service you offer |
| **Image and text** | Photo with a stat card next to text, a checklist and buttons |
| **Neighborhoods** | Photo tiles for the areas you cover |
| **Agents** | Team cards with photo, role, phone and email |
| **Client reviews** | Star ratings and quotes from buyers and sellers |
| **How it works** | Numbered steps, for example how selling with you works |
| **Valuation form** | Text with a checklist next to a HubSpot form |
| **Mortgage calculator** | Monthly payment calculator with price, down payment, rate and term |
| **FAQ** | Accordion of questions and answers |
| **Call to action banner** | Dark banner with a photo, a heading and two buttons |
