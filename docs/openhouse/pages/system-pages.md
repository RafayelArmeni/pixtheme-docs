# System pages

System pages are the pages HubSpot shows in special situations. Openhouse styles all of them to match your site.

| Template | Where to choose it |
|---|---|
| **404 Page not found** and **500 Server error** | Settings › Content › Pages › System pages |
| **Search results** and **Password prompt** | Settings › Content › Pages › System pages |
| **Email subscription preferences**, **Backup unsubscribe**, **Subscription update confirmation** | Settings › Marketing › Email › Subscriptions |
| **Membership login**, **register**, **reset password request** and **reset password** | Settings › Content › Private content › Templates |

The 404 and 500 pages use the **Error Page** module: edit its heading, text, search field, buttons and helpful links in the page editor.
