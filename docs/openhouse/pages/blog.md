# Blog

Openhouse includes a **Blog listing** and a **Blog post** template.

## Use them for your blog

1. Go to **Settings › Content › Blog**, choose your blog, and open **Templates**.
2. Set **Blog listing page** to Openhouse **Blog listing** and **Blog post template** to Openhouse **Blog post**.
3. Save.

## Blog listing

* On page one, the newest post is shown large, then a grid of cards.
* Topic buttons at the top filter posts by topic. Topic and author pages use the same template.
* A newsletter box with your blog subscription form.
* A banner below the posts. It sits in a drag-and-drop area: edit it in the blog listing editor, swap it for another module, or remove it.

## Blog post

* Breadcrumb, author, date and reading time.
* Cover image from the post's featured image.
* Share buttons (Facebook, LinkedIn, X and copy link) that stay in view while reading.
* Topics, an author box, comments (when turned on for the blog) and three related posts from the same topic.

Turn each part on or off in **Theme settings › Blog**.
