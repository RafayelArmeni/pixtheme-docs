# Header, footer and menus

The header and footer are **global content**: edit them once and they change on every page.

## Create your menus

1. Go to **Content › Website pages**, click the gear icon for settings, then **Navigation**. (Or search "Navigation menus" in HubSpot.)
2. Create a **main menu** for the header. Add your pages, and drag pages under others to make dropdowns, for example *Buy › Homes for sale, Homes for rent*.
3. Create two smaller menus for the footer columns, for example *Properties* and *Company*.

## Edit the header

Open any Openhouse page, click the header, then **Open global content editor**.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Logo** | Your logo, its height and link. Leave the logo empty to use the logo from your brand settings |
| **Menu** | Choose your main menu and how many levels to show |
| **Phone number** | Shows your phone number with a label on wide screens |
| **Header button** | The button on the right, for example *Book a valuation* |
| **Top bar** | An optional dark bar above the header with an announcement, email and office hours |
| **Language switcher** | Shows a language menu when the page has translations |

The header can stay at the top while visitors scroll: turn on **Sticky header** in theme settings under **Header**.

## Edit the footer

The footer has your logo, a short text, social links, menu columns (add as many as you need) and your office details (address, phone, email and opening hours), plus a bottom bar with the copyright text, legal links and a **legal notice**.

!!! tip

    Use the footer's **Legal notice** for your broker license number, fair housing statement or listing disclaimer.
