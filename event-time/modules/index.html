    All modules¶

    Find any module in the page editor's Add panel by searching for its name. Each module has a Content tab for text and images and a Style tab (see Section style and the Style tab).

    Module What it's for Details
    Hero Opening section with event name, date, location, buttons and countdown Hero
    Page intro Opening section for inner pages, with breadcrumbs Page intro
    Coming soon Full-screen teaser with countdown and waitlist form Coming soon
    Schedule tabs Agenda with one tab per day Schedule tabs
    Countdown Large countdown with a button Countdown
    Marquee strip Endless scrolling band of large text Marquee strip
    Speakers Speaker grid with photos and social links Speakers
    Testimonials Quotes in 5 layouts with ratings and video Testimonials

    The Site header and Site footer are covered in Header and footer.

    Fields many modules share¶

    • Section heading: eyebrow text (small label above), heading, heading level (H1, H2 or H3), intro text and alignment (left or center). Use only one H1 per page, usually in the hero or page intro.
    • Buttons: text, link and style (Primary (gradient), Outline or White). A button with empty text is hidden.
    • Anchor ID: lets you link to the section, for example #schedule. Use it for "View schedule" buttons on the same page.
    • Labels: small texts for screen readers and buttons. Translate them if your site isn't in English.
